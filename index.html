ABOUT

Henry Finn is a frontier storyteller in blockchain, AI, and crypto entertainment. He brings culture to the frontier and builds community around IP and entertainment.

In 2023 he led the acquisition of 0N1 Force, the first anime franchise of scale from crypto. As CEO, he is rebuilding the franchise for AI.

He operates GK Labs alongside 0N1 Force: an IP and entertainment incubator in OFR Fund, founded by Wei Zhou, former CFO of Binance.

Fifteen years of production: digital cinema, VR, AR, and 360 video, then blockchain and AI.

As president of Luminous US, an East-West production company, he produced for Google, Facebook, Samsung, Spotify, Activision, Nike, and Walmart, and for Johnny Depp, Jamie Foxx, and Steph Curry. Blueprint Media, through which he produced for technology companies and investors, was acquired by Luminous Seoul.

Anime Cinema Club is a public anime screening series.

Award-winning activist. He has worked with many nonprofits, Andrew Yang, and Love Not Blood.

He builds East-West franchises that travel: Hollywood, anime, gaming, and frontier technology.
