ABOUT

Henry Finn is an artistpreneur and investor. He is a co-founder and general partner of GK Labs, an IP technology fund and incubator for talent, part of OFR Fund, founded by Wei Zhou, former CFO of Binance. He develops projects with major IPs from East and West.

He is building toward what he calls a layer 2 society, a digital society that lives on top of the real world as its own community and economy. Governments, schools, and other real-world institutions stay as they are, and the added economy gives its citizens another layer of support. He uses the term decentralized autonomous nation for the same idea, a digital nation powered by AI and built on blockchain.

Five of his companies are in the GK Labs portfolio. He founded Unicorn Studios, which makes films for technology companies and founders, and Metacomica, a comic book publisher that brings Japanese and Korean IP to Western readers in American comic format. In 2023 he led the acquisition of 0N1 Force, the first anime franchise of scale from crypto, and he is its CEO. He co-founded Enclave Studios, which builds original East-West universes with Paul Jenkins, Ko Mori, and Bryan Li, and Executive Intelligence.

Outside GK Labs, he is Chief Innovation Officer at Animori, a digital collectibles platform for the anime industry, and he hosts and produces Silicon Valley Insider, long-form interviews with the people building AI infrastructure. He also runs Anime Cinema Club, a public anime screening series.

He has produced for fifteen years, starting in digital cinema and moving through VR, AR, and 360 video into blockchain and AI. As president of Luminous US, an East-West production company, he produced for Google, Facebook, Samsung, Spotify, Activision, Nike, and Walmart, and for Johnny Depp, Jamie Foxx, and Steph Curry. Blueprint Media, through which he produced for technology companies and investors, was acquired by Luminous Seoul.

He is an award-winning activist and has worked with many nonprofits, Andrew Yang, and Love Not Blood.
