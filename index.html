ABOUT

Henry Finn is a founder at the intersection of story and technology. He builds companies and IP in AI, blockchain, VR/AR, and entertainment, and develops projects with major IPs from East and West.

He founded Unicorn Studios, which makes films for technology companies and founders, and he hosts and produces Silicon Valley Insider, long-form interviews with the people building AI infrastructure. He is Chief Innovation Officer at Animori, a digital collectibles platform for the anime industry, and he founded Metacomica, a comic book publisher that brings Japanese and Korean IP to Western readers in American comic format.

He has produced for fifteen years, starting in digital cinema and moving through VR, AR, and 360 video into blockchain and AI.

As president of Luminous US, an East-West production company, he produced for Google, Facebook, Samsung, Spotify, Activision, Nike, and Walmart, and for Johnny Depp, Jamie Foxx, and Steph Curry. Blueprint Media, through which he produced for technology companies and investors, was acquired by Luminous Seoul.

In 2023 he led the acquisition of 0N1 Force, the first anime franchise of scale from crypto, and he is its CEO. He also operates GK Labs, an IP and entertainment incubator in OFR Fund, founded by Wei Zhou, former CFO of Binance.

He runs Anime Cinema Club, a public anime screening series. He is an award-winning activist and has worked with many nonprofits, Andrew Yang, and Love Not Blood.
